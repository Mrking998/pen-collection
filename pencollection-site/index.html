The Short Version

Why Imported

Pen Collection exists because well tailored corporate wear for women wasn't easy to find locally. We mean the kind that actually fits and holds its shape. So we source it directly, bringing in blazers, skirt suits and pantsuits from overseas in small runs.

Every shipment is limited. What's in the shop this month sells out and isn't always restocked the same way.

Our Guarantee

Every piece is inspected before sale. If something's not as described when you collect it in store, tell us right away and we'll make it right. That means a straight swap where the size or piece is available.
