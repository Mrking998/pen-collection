Our Story

Why we go looking for it ourselves.

Pen Collection started with a simple frustration: well-cut, properly finished corporate wear for women was hard to find in Lagos. Ready-to-wear suits either fit oddly, wore out fast, or just didn't exist in the colours we wanted. So instead of waiting for someone else to bring them in, we started doing it ourselves.

Today, that means sourcing blazers, skirt suits and pantsuits directly and importing them in small, deliberate batches. Not container-loads of the same three colours, but a considered range that changes with every shipment. We check each piece for fit and finish before it ever reaches the rack, and we sell everything in person, from our two shops, so you can see and try before you commit.

✓

Our Guarantee

Every piece is inspected before it goes on sale. If something isn't as described when you collect it in-store, tell us right away. That covers a wrong size sent aside or a finishing fault we missed. We'll sort it out, including a straight swap where the size or piece is available. Full details are in our Terms of Service.
