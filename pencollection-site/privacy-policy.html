Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

This policy explains what information Pen Collection Nig. LTD. ("Pen Collection", "we", "us") collects through this website, and how we use it. We are a physical retail business. All sales happen in our shops, not on this website.

Information We Collect

  • Information you give us directly: your name, phone number, and message content, when you use the contact form on this site or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Cookies and similar technology: if you accept our cookie banner, we may use basic cookies to remember your preference and to understand how visitors use the site (for example, through analytics tools). You can decline non-essential cookies from the banner shown on your first visit.
  • Information from your device: standard technical information such as browser type and general location, which most websites collect automatically for security and performance purposes.

How We Use It

  • To respond to enquiries sent through the contact form or WhatsApp.
  • To understand how people use the website, so we can improve it.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Contact Form & WhatsApp

Our contact form does not submit to a server. It opens a pre-filled message in WhatsApp for you to send yourself. That message, and any reply, is handled through WhatsApp under Meta's own privacy terms in addition to this policy.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except where required by law, or with service providers who help us run this website (for example, hosting or analytics providers), under confidentiality obligations.

Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask us to delete it. To do this, contact us at jacket.md@gmail.com.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to jacket.md@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at 0893 079 0672.
